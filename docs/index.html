v3.0.3 Oct 13, 2026

Two-way voice works, with real profile pictures and the new Focus call layout. Lark adds the Feed dashboard, a soundboard, per-user volume, a replay clip recorder, and an in-app store.

Added

  • Focus voice layout: a short stage above chat with the screen share or active speaker and the call controls, folding into the sidebar while you read. (#169)
  • Feed dashboard: one activity list of streams, games, music, voice joins, and friend requests with Watch, Join, and Accept buttons, on Home or beside chat. (#170)
  • Guild soundboard you can play and hear in voice. (#164)
  • Echo cancellation toggle beside noise suppression. (#167)
  • Per-user volume and Mute for you in the voice participant menu. (#172)
  • Replay clip recorder: Ctrl+Shift+C saves the last 15 seconds to 2 minutes with hardware encoding, separate game and mic audio, and game-named files. (#175)
  • Check for updates button in Settings. (#153)
  • Signed interface bundles with Stable and Canary channels and automatic rollback if a bundle does not boot. (#154, #159, #168)
  • In-app store in Settings to install, update, and uninstall plugins and themes. New plugins start off. (#171)
  • Game desk plugins: session clock, squad and build cards, and catalogs for Overwatch 2, Deadlock, Call of Duty, and GTA V. None touch the game process. (#162, #165, #174)

Changed

  • The update banner reads Restart to finish the update, and an update waits until a voice call ends. (#153)
  • Website, progress page, and store restyled in Minimal Mono. Finished 3.0 work folds into version history. (#160, #166)
  • Voice join is logged step by step and shows a timeout instead of stalling. (#176)

Fixed

  • Inbound voice decrypts Discord RTP header extensions, so other people are audible, and packet loss is counted correctly. (#156, #177)
  • Outbound voice sends once DAVE is ready and the mic clears the activity gate. (#163, #177)
  • Voice participants show their Discord avatars instead of letters in the sidebar and on call tiles. (#155)
  • Recorded clips play in the embedded player, and new clips are saved in a format that plays everywhere. (#173)
  • Restored the input gain test and updated progress page tests. (#157, #158, #161)
